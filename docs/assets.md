- kitchen.webp: British house kitchen, sage shaker cabinets, oak dining table, skylight and garden doors.
- bedroom.webp: British bedroom, sash window, upholstered bed, linen bedding, oak bedside tables and natural light.
- terraces.webp: fictional British Victorian terrace with leafy gardens; used on landlord information and a non-homepage example listing.
